Jamaica Picks
TVJ's national prediction game for the FIFA World Cup 2026. Here's how it works:
1
Make your picksTap the team you think wins each match, in every round from the group stage to the final. Change your mind any time before kickoff. Once a match starts, picks for it are closed.
2
Picks stay sealedNobody sees the running totals, not even you. Every pick joins Jamaica's national tally behind the scenes.
3
The big revealBefore kickoff, TVJ reveals what Jamaica picked, on air and right here in the app. Did the country back your team?
4
Score pointsWhen the final whistle blows, every correct pick earns you a point. Your running score lives at the top of the screen for the whole tournament.

Share itTap Share to get your personal picks card with your Instagram name on it. Post it to Instagram and tag @televisionjamaica so Jamaica sees your picks. Watch every match on TVJ, stream on 1SpotMedia, listen on FAME 95 FM.
